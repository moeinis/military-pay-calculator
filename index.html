Estimate only. Models 2026 base pay (incl. Executive Schedule caps), BAS, federal tax, FICA (with the Additional Medicare Tax), and state income tax using real 2026 brackets & standard deductions, and combat-zone exclusion (enlisted & warrant officers fully excluded; commissioned officers capped at max enlisted pay + IDP). BAH auto-fills from official 2026 DoD (DTMO) rates by duty station, grade, and dependents — override anytime. State tax credits, itemized deductions, and local taxes outside Maryland are not modeled. Drill and annual-training pay for non-mobilized Guard and Reserve members is not modeled, and some states tax Guard pay differently from active-duty pay.
