Estimated pay

Federal income tax is estimated as annual tax liability ÷ 12 using 2026 brackets and the standard deduction — actual paycheck withholding may differ.

Estimate only. Models 2026 base pay (incl. Executive Schedule caps), BAS, federal tax, FICA (with the Additional Medicare Tax), and state income tax using real 2026 brackets & standard deductions, and combat-zone exclusion (enlisted & warrant officers fully excluded; commissioned officers capped at max enlisted pay + IDP). BAH auto-fills from official 2026 DoD (DTMO) rates by duty station, grade, and dependents — override anytime. State tax credits, itemized deductions, and local taxes outside Maryland are not modeled. Drill and annual-training pay for non-mobilized Guard and Reserve members is not modeled, and some states tax Guard pay differently from active-duty pay. Verify with your finance office / MyPay before making financial decisions.
